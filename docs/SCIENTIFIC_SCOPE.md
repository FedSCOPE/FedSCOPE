# Scientific scope and interpretation

## Supported empirical question

Given measured simultaneous energy traces and a counterfactual emergency-sharing market, can a focal participant preserve its maximum attainable current service while making its public priority history less statistically dependent on a causal private anticipated-distress state?

## Primary reference result

For `DE_KN_industrial3`, 24-hour anticipated distress, and 1,639 genuine game-active states:

- minimum-service anticipated dCor: 0.107813;
- FedSCOPE anticipated dCor: 0.047258;
- relative reduction: 56.2%;
- service difference versus minimum service: 0 kWh;
- aggregate game-active signal increase versus minimum service: 5.03%;
- realized-future dCor: 0.105123 -> 0.058024 (44.8% ex-post reduction).

Horizon sensitivity gives anticipated-dCor reductions of 64.9% at 6 h, 59.2% at 12 h, and 56.2% at 24 h, with zero service loss in each case.

## Heterogeneity warning

Focal rotation is not uniformly favorable. The strong primary result should not be presented as a universal microgrid property. The available opacity headroom depends strongly on the focal signal/deficit process and the structure of the minimum-service floor.

## What the result is not

- It is not differential privacy.
- It is not a cryptographic protocol.
- It is not a proof of independence.
- It is not a physical distribution-network power-flow study.
- It is not an observed historical emergency market.
- It does not show that federated forecasting universally beats all local or seasonal baselines.
